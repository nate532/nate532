## INPUTS TO VERIFY

For each item, record **confirmed, open, or not applicable**, with the responsible person and a supporting reference. An unchecked item is unresolved, not assumed complete.

- [ ] **Scope and drawings:** Work limits, drawing/specification references, revision dates, and open clarifications identified.
- [ ] **Prerequisites:** Preceding work, dependencies, and required decisions identified; completion evidence recorded.
- [ ] **Materials:** Required items, quantities, availability, delivery timing, and outstanding substitutions reviewed.
- [ ] **Equipment:** Required equipment, availability, and responsible coordinator identified.
- [ ] **Access and permits:** Access arrangements and applicable permit requirements verified by the responsible person; verification date, reference, and remaining conditions recorded.
- [ ] **Interfaces:** Affected teams, shared areas, sequencing, and handoff points identified.
